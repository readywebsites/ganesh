## 🌟 Overview

Welcome to the official digital portal for **Surat Cha Gaurinandan Ganesh Mahotsav 2026** (Nandanvan 2, Vesu, Surat).

This application transforms the digital festival into a physical walk through an ancient Indian temple, culminating in the divine darshan and blessings of Lord Ganesha.

---

## 🏛️ The 7-Scene Narrative Journey

1. **Scene 1 • Morning Sky**: Soft moving clouds, morning fog, flying birds, and gentle ambient temple soundscapes.
2. **Scene 2 • Sanctuary Approach**: Camera glides toward the massive temple facade as golden sunlight hits the structure.
3. **Scene 3 • Temple Entrance**: Double carved wooden doors slowly open dynamically as the user scrolls.
4. **Scene 4 • Sacred Corridor**: Walkthrough of golden pillars, dark polished marble, oil lamps (diyas), incense smoke, and hanging temple bells.
5. **Scene 5 • Garbh Gruh**: Entrance into the inner sanctum with sole focus on Lord Ganesha.
6. **Scene 6 • Divine Aura**: Soft volumetric light, god rays, bloom, and golden halo encircling the idol.
7. **Scene 7 • Divine Blessing**: Extreme close-up of Lord Ganesha in Abhaya Mudra blessing pose.

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [Next.js 14](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router)
- **3D Engine**: [React Three Fiber](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/) & [Three.js](https://threejs.org/)
- **3D Helpers & Postprocessing**: [@react-three/drei](https://github.com/pmndrs/drei) & [@react-three/postprocessing](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-postprocessing)
- **Animations**: [GSAP](https://gsap.com/) & [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
- **Smooth Scroll**: [Lenis](https://lenis.darkroom.engineering/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) & Custom CSS Variables
